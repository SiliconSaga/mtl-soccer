How MTL Soccer Works

New to MTL Soccer? Here’s how the program works from registration to game day.

Registration

Registration opens before each season (spring, summer, fall). You’ll sign up through TeamSnap, which is the platform MTL uses to manage teams, schedules, and communication — Fall 2026 registration is open now.

Early-bird pricing is typically available for a limited window before the season starts — see the home page for current rates. Watch for announcements on Facebook and Instagram.

Age Groups & Formats

Age Group Grades Format Day
Little Kickers Pre-K/K 4v4 (no goalie) Varies
1st/2nd Grade 1st-2nd 4v4 (no goalie) Varies
3rd/4th Grade 3rd-4th 7v7 (with goalie) Sunday
5th/6th Grade 5th-6th 9v9 (with goalie) Sunday

See the rules pages for detailed standards of play: Little Kickers, 1st/2nd Grade, and 3rd–6th Grade.

How Teams Are Built

After registration closes, MTL organizers assign players to teams. You’ll receive a TeamSnap invitation for your child’s team with your coach’s contact information.

  • Teams are assigned by the league — you cannot request a specific team
  • Coaches are volunteers, often parents from the team
  • If you’re interested in coaching, let MTL know during registration or email MTLsoccer@gmail.com

Schedules & Time Slots

Game schedules are published in TeamSnap before the season starts. Your coach will share the schedule with the team.

How time slots work: Some age groups have enough teams for multiple time slots on game day. Your team will be assigned to a specific slot — but it may vary from week to week. For example, one week your team might play at 9:00 AM and the next at 10:30 AM. Always check TeamSnap for your upcoming game time.

Some teams may have a consistent weekly time while others rotate between slots depending on the number of teams and field availability.

The Season

A typical season runs 8-10 weeks. Each week:

  • Little Kickers — 25 minutes of practice followed by a 25-minute game
  • 1st/2nd Grade — two 20-minute halves
  • 3rd-6th Grade — two 25-minute halves

Games are held at fields around West Orange — see Field Locations for maps and parking.
