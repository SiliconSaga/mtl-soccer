Liberty is one of the sites used for MTL Soccer's 3rd/4th and 5th/6th Coed games in West Orange, NJ. The fields are behind the school, on its south side.

## Getting There

[Open in Google Maps](https://maps.app.goo.gl/mjaURzf7jreXEq6p9)

Field placement at Liberty varies enough from season to season that there's no overhead layout here yet — check TeamSnap for your exact field assignment before heading out.

## Parking

- Park in the school lot, favoring the **south end** — closest to the fields
- If the lot is full, there may be street parking on **Kelly Drive**, with a staircase up to the fields from there

## Tips for Parents

- The bathroom situation is unknown — plan accordingly
- **Arrive early** — parking can be challenging with hundreds of kids across programs
