## Overview

3rd/4th Coed and 5th/6th Coed both play **7v7 with a goalie** on the larger fields at [Liberty]({{ site.baseurl }}/soccer/fields/liberty/) and [O'Connor Park]({{ site.baseurl }}/soccer/fields/oconnor/).

| Age Group | Format | League |
|---|---|---|
| **3rd/4th Grade** | 7v7 (6 + goalie) | 3/4 Coed |
| **5th/6th Grade** | 7v7 (6 + goalie) | 5/6 Coed |

Each time slot is a full game of **two 25-minute halves** with a 3-minute halftime. A teenage referee (sometimes two) oversees the game and keeps time.

Games start at the appointed time — teams are welcome to arrive 15 minutes early to warm up.

A larger or smaller number of players can be used if agreed upon by both coaches and the referee. The referee has the deciding vote.

## Players

- Shin guards are **required** — ask your coach if you need a pair
- Cleats are recommended but not required
- **Goalie gloves are provided** by the league
- Substitutions are **unlimited** and can occur at any stoppage

## Ball In and Out of Play

- **Kick-offs, free kicks, goal kicks, and corner kicks** are used to start or restart play
- **Throw-ins** are used when the ball goes out over the sideline
  - In the **first two weeks** of the season, incorrect throw-ins are **repeatable** — coaches and referees should provide guidance
- Goal kicks and corner kicks should be taken in the general vicinity of the respective goal or corner if field lines are not apparent

## Restarts

- Opponents must be at least **10 feet away** from the ball on all restarts (except goal kicks)
- On **goal kicks**, the opposing team must return to **their side of the field** (behind the midfield line) — coaches and referees need to enforce this on every occasion

## Heading and Offside

These two rules are where 3rd/4th and 5th/6th differ:

| | 3rd/4th Grade | 5th/6th Grade |
|---|---|---|
| **Heading** | Not allowed — indirect free kick to the opposing team | Allowed |
| **Offside** | Not tracked | Enforced — this division introduces the concept |

Coaches and referees need to enforce the 3rd/4th no-heading rule on every occasion.

## Important Rules

- **No penalty kicks**

## Scores and Standings

- The referee tracks the score, confirms the final result with each coach, and reports it to [MTLsoccer@gmail.com](mailto:MTLsoccer@gmail.com) by Sunday evening
- Scores and standings are kept in **TeamSnap** for these divisions
- With enough teams, **3rd/4th plays semi-finals and finals** at the end of the season

## After the Game

- Teams and coaches **must shake hands** with the other players, coaches, and referees after the game

## Field Safety

- Fields are lined by the town; dimensions may vary slightly from the recommended 55-65 x 35-45 yards
- Corner flags are not required
- **Goals must be anchored** by sand bags (provided by the town)
- Referees and coaches are responsible for ensuring a safe playing field before kick-off

## Coaches & Parents

Together, coaches and parents are expected to create and promote a **fun and safe environment** for the players.

- **Coaches stay on the sideline**, not the field — running up and down the side is fine. Referees are the ones on the field.
- **No one stands behind the goal**, coaches or parents — it intimidates the kids.
- Practices are optional and arranged through the league — see the [league guidelines]({{ site.baseurl }}/soccer/coach-guide/#league-guidelines) in the Coach Guide.

Inappropriate conduct on or off the field will be addressed by MTL Trustees and board members. Issues must be reported to [MTLsoccer@gmail.com](mailto:MTLsoccer@gmail.com).
