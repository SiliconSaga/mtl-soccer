## Registration & Teams

**When does registration open?**
Registration opens before each season (spring, summer, fall). Watch for
announcements on [Facebook](https://www.facebook.com/mountaintopleague),
[Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/mountain_top_league), and the
[Mountain Top League website](https://mountaintopleague.com/).

**Can I request a specific team or coach?**
No — teams are assigned by MTL organizers after registration closes. Your
child will play with the team they are assigned to.

**Can siblings be on the same team?**
This is not guaranteed. Contact
[MTLsoccer@gmail.com](mailto:MTLsoccer@gmail.com) if you have a specific
concern.

## Equipment & Uniforms

**What does the coach provide?**
The coach provides a uniform (jersey, shorts, socks). For Little Kickers and
1st/2nd Grade, the coach also provides shin guards.

**Do I need to buy cleats?**
Cleats are recommended but not required — sneakers are fine.

**What size soccer ball does my child need?**
Size 3 or 4 for ages 4-8 (Little Kickers and 1st/2nd Grade). Size 4 or 5 for
ages 8-12 (3rd-6th Grade).

**Can we reuse last season's uniform?**
Yes — the uniforms are the same as previous seasons. If you have the shorts,
socks, and shin guards from last season, your child can wear those.

## Game Day

**What if it rains?**
Games are played in the rain. Only thunder, lightning, or unsafe field
conditions will cancel a game. Cancellations are decided by MTL at the league
level — individual coaches cannot cancel. MTL will try to email within 45
minutes of the session time.

**Can my child wear a hat?**
No — baseball hats are not allowed during play for safety reasons (the brim
can hit other children).

**Can I bring my dog?**
No — dogs are not allowed on or around the field of play.

**Where do I park?**
See the [Field Locations]({{ site.baseurl }}/soccer/fields/) page for
parking details specific to each field.

## During the Season

**My child can't make a game — what do I do?**
Update your availability in [TeamSnap]({{ site.baseurl }}/soccer/teamsnap/)
so your coach knows.

**I have a concern about a coach, player, or parent — who do I contact?**
Email [MTLsoccer@gmail.com](mailto:MTLsoccer@gmail.com). All concerns are
handled by the MTL Trustees.

**I'd like to help — how do I volunteer?**
Email [MTLsoccer@gmail.com](mailto:MTLsoccer@gmail.com). Coaches, referees,
and general volunteers are always welcome — no prior experience required.
